# Resources

## Protected handed-over input

`Build your own Tokenizer.pdf` is the challenge proposal handed to the learner by the CEO of CodeCrafters. Keep it byte-for-byte unchanged. Analyses, corrections, and proposed stage revisions belong under `docs/`, not inside the source PDF.

Current inventory facts: the document is titled “Build your own Tokenizer,” describes a 16-stage byte-level BPE challenge, and includes proposed behavior, examples, tests, scope boundaries, and references. These facts do not imply that this workspace has approved its design.

## Research source list

`sources.md` is a curated learning sequence, not proof that the learner has completed each source. It records the teaching role and review state of each link, prioritizes primary or authoritative material, and marks weaker or redundant sources honestly.

## Provenance rule

For every added resource, record who supplied it or where it was found, the access date, the question it helps answer, and whether it has been reviewed. Do not silently rewrite externally supplied artifacts.
